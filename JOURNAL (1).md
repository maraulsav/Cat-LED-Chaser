So, what I did to disattach the LED from PCB is that
1. Add more soldering iron to the pin.. I know this can be sound confusing but the explanation is that when you add more soldering iron when it is heat up the pin is loosened up better rather when it contain few soldering iron.. I think it is because soldering iron when heated became a liquid and it is esier to transfer heat when there is more soldering iron component on the pin.

2. Heat up the soldering iron and push the component. If you have a helping hand I suggest you to use it.. Avoid direct contact. As the pin really hot and also there is a chance where the solder accidentally get touched by your skin (This is what happen to me btw, so yea be careful)

![Screenshot 2026-03-17 at 15.01.03](https://stasis.hackclub-assets.com/images/1773734469461-jx831p.png)

After that, I designed the PCB with the shape of a Cat..
How do I built the cat design? 
So, first I search for a cat design in the internet, that I import the picture as a foot print using the image converter in KiCad. After that, I imported the foot print and use edge cut line to determine the outer line. Oh, and my recommendation is that you have to set the edge cut lines to 2 mm, because when I used the default the edge cut of 0.5 mm, it didn't work because of ambigous line intersection.. (but it can be perhaps because of my skills). 

After you trace all of that, place the component inside of the trace. I recommend you to place your microcontroller first, because the other components placement depend on it, as they need to take the shortest way possible.. This is just my recommendation so that the wiring part is clearer. After wiring, I decorated  the PCB using footprints pictures, like the stars, eyes, nose.
